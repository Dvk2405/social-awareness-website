Child Labour – Concept and Implications

Child labour refers to the employment of children in work that deprives them of their childhood, education, and proper development. It is a serious social problem and a violation of children’s rights.

Causes

  • Poverty
  • Lack of education
  • Unemployment of parents
  • Demand for cheap labour

Consequences

  • Loss of education
  • Health problems
  • Poverty cycle
  • Violation of human rights

Remedial Measures

  • Strict child labour laws
  • Free education
  • Awareness programs
  • Financial support for poor families

Women Empowerment and Its Importance

Women empowerment means giving women the rights, opportunities, and confidence to make decisions in personal, social, and economic life.

  • Promotes gender equality
  • Encourages education and employment
  • Improves family welfare
  • Leads to national development

E-Waste – Problems and Sustainable Management

E-waste includes discarded electronic devices like computers, mobiles, TVs, and batteries.

Problems

  • Toxic substances like lead and mercury
  • Environmental pollution
  • Health risks
  • Loss of valuable metals

Management Strategies

  • Recycling
  • Reuse and refurbishment
  • Public awareness
  • Government regulations
  • Producer responsibility

Importance of Voting Rights and Human Rights

Voting Rights

Voting allows citizens to choose their representatives and participate in democratic governance. It ensures accountability and strengthens democracy.

Human Rights

Human rights include the right to life, freedom, equality, and education. They protect individuals from injustice and discrimination.
